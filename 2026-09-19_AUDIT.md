## 3. Wrapper-replacement check

- This repo's three rules (`genbank_to_replicon`, `fasta_gff3_to_replicon`,
  `combine_annotations`) are custom Biopython/gffutils logic with no
  wrapper equivalent.
- The `bowtie2_build` rule lives in the separate `bowtie2_build_index_workflow`
  submodule and could use the official `bio/bowtie2/build` snakemake-wrapper

  (submodule has its own repo); flag skipped from this repo's action items.

## 5. Module/submodule consumption — pass, with one note

Both submodules are consumed via `use rule * from <module> as <prefix>_*`
with namespaced config passed through the module's `config:` field —
matches the recommended pattern, no cherry-picking.

`make_IGV_genome_workflow` is pinned to tag `v0.1.1`.
`bowtie2_build_index_workflow` has no upstream tags; its gitlink in this
repo's tree already records the exact commit `c89f3c2`, and `.gitmodules`
has no `branch =` entry, so there is no live tracking/drift risk from this
repo's side — `git submodule update --remote` would move it, but nothing
here does that automatically. The `(heads/main)` label from `git submodule
status` is descriptive of what branch that commit happens to be the tip of,
not evidence of an active float. No change made in this repo; if stronger
pinning is wanted, it would mean tagging a release in the
`bowtie2_build_index_workflow` repo itself and re-pointing the gitlink to
that tag — left for a future decision.
